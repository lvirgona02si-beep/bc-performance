The Coach

My Journey In Combat Sports Performance

My success in Muay Thai wasn’t built on fight skills alone. It came from taking every aspect of performance seriously: strength and conditioning, nutrition and recovery.

That same philosophy is the foundation of BC Performance & Nutrition.

As both an athlete and coach, I’ve worked with combat sports athletes from beginners through to professionals, helping them improve their strength, conditioning, nutrition and overall performance.

I now bring that experience directly into gyms, delivering sport specific coaching designed around the demands of combat sports, making quality strength and conditioning coaching accessible at an affordable price.

My goal is simple: help athletes train smarter, perform better and reach their full potential without breaking the bank.

My Mission

Helping Combat Athletes Reach Their Potential

My mission is to give more people who train in combat sports the knowledge, coaching, and support they need to get the most out of every session both inside and outside the gym, while making that support as accessible and affordable as possible.

Through BC Performance & Nutrition, I bring sport-specific strength and conditioning directly into gyms, alongside guidance on nutrition, mindset, recovery and mobility. I help athletes understand how these areas work together so they can make informed decisions about their training and recovery.

From first-time beginners to competitive amateurs, my goal is to help people build strength, improve conditioning and develop habits that support lasting progress inside and outside the gym.
