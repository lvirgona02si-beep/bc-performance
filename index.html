The Coach

My Journey In Combat Sports Performance

I went from a complete beginner to WBC state champion, national champion and ranked top 20 in the world — in four years. I didn't get there by only training fight skills. I got there by treating every pillar of performance as non-negotiable: strength and conditioning, nutrition, mindset, recovery and fight analysis.

That's the exact system I now bring into gyms through BC Performance & Nutrition — sport-specific strength and conditioning, coached directly, built around the demands of combat sports. I work with everyone from first-time beginners through to competitive amateurs.
